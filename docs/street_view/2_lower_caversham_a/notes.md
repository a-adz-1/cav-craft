# Notes

- Panoramas in the hidden browser pane often showed black or a grey loading screen after 5 s. Fix used for most views: navigate, wait 5 s, a throwaway 0.1-scale screenshot (forces a frame), wait 4 s, then the saved 0.6-scale screenshot. Some early views were retried more than once because of this rendering glitch, not because the panorama was missing.
- Early Gosbrook Road / Henley Road views (before that fix) are blurrier low-res tiles.
- Several batches failed with "Browser pane is not displayed"; those views were re-taken individually.
- Some later screenshots are 480x449 (the pane was resized mid-run).
- Location oddities: Alston Walk and Rothwell Walk views sit on Charles Evans Way / Caleta Close (the Walks are footpaths); George Street 2_* is the George Street / Vastern Road junction south of the river, in central Reading.
- No views were skipped; every view has a saved screenshot.
